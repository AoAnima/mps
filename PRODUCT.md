# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS — no build framework. Icons via Font Awesome or Lucide (CDN); fonts via Google Fonts (Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans).

## Users

Primary user: a crypto-native buyer and collector browsing a Web3 marketplace to discover and purchase digital and physical goods (art, game skins, clothing/physical items, music) with cryptocurrency. Their situation: scan trending drops, check 24h top sales and price movement, compare crypto and fiat price, then connect a wallet to buy.

## Product Purpose

A multi-chain marketplace for discovering and buying digital and physical goods with cryptocurrency. The home page is the discovery surface: a featured drop, a 24-hour top-sales widget, category filters, and a browsable product grid — all with dual crypto/fiat pricing and wallet-based checkout.

## Positioning

Multi-chain Web3 commerce (Polygon, Ethereum, Arbitrum) presented in a dark, futuristic, glassmorphic interface that keeps e-commerce legible. Transparent dual pricing and curated discovery distinguish it from the category-default marketplace.

## Operating Context

Web3/NFT commerce. A visitor selects a blockchain network, connects a wallet, searches products/collections/addresses, filters by category, scans 24h top sales with price movement, and buys in crypto with a shown fiat equivalent. A footer risk disclaimer accompanies crypto-asset trading.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single home-page interface; semantic HTML5 (`header`, `nav`, `main`, `section`, `article`, `footer`).
- Mobile-first responsive: custom burger menu on mobile, tablet, and desktop up to 1920px.
- Pure CSS / Flexbox / CSS Grid; no heavy frameworks. Icons and fonts via CDN.
- Dual pricing (crypto + fiat equivalent) and a per-listing network badge.
- Undecided: the marketplace's name and logo (a minimalist placeholder is acceptable for the first build).

## Brand Commitments

Binding visual direction volunteered by the user: Dark Futuristic with Glassmorphism (matte-glass cards and panels via `backdrop-filter: blur`). Palette: deep dark `#0B0B0F` background, graphite `#16161F` controls, neon purple `#7C3AED` accent, cyber green `#10B981` for status/success. Typography: Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans. (Recorded as constraints; the full visual world is established in DESIGN.md.)

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real product imagery, listings, prices, testimonials, or brand assets exist yet. The first build must use placeholder imagery and clearly sample (non-fabricated-looking) data. Do not invent testimonials, customers, benchmarks, or deployment claims.

## Product Principles

1. Discovery first — search, categories, and top sales make browsing effortless.
2. Transparent value — every price shows crypto and fiat; price movement is visible.
3. Legible calm — a premium, clean, intuitive e-commerce surface despite the dense, dark aesthetic.
4. Respect the chain — network selection and wallet connection are first-class controls.
5. Honest about risk — crypto-asset risk is disclosed, not hidden.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive and semantic by requirement. No specific accessibility standard was confirmed; mobile-first behavior and a clear hierarchy are the baseline.
